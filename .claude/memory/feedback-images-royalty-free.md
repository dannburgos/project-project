Dann said (2026-11-07) that all the visual resources she uses are royalty-free or AI-generated, so copyright is not a reason to hold back, ask about, or exclude images from commits or pushes, even though the repo is public.

**Why:** I had hesitated to publish PetRide photos and the Cabify, Uber and inDrive logos because they might carry rights, and it cost her a confirmation round.

**How to apply:**

- Commit and push case images (photos, logos, covers) without asking about rights.
- Size is a separate matter: keep out of commits the heavy originals (e.g. the 394 MB `public/petride/img ` folder) and publish web-optimized copies instead.
- Replaces the earlier rule about keeping third-party reference images out of commits, see [[feedback-branch-per-feature]].
