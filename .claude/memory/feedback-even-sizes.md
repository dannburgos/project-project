Sizes must always be multiples of 2 px (font sizes especially: 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 32…). When she asks to make something "un poco más grande", go to the next even step.

**Why:** Her design rule for the portfolio; she reminded me explicitly ("Recuerda que siempre en múltiplos de 2").

**How to apply:** Never propose or set odd pixel values (e.g. 15px, 17px, 21px). Check Tailwind classes map to even px. Related: [[project-case-studies]]
